# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads define the quality strategy, validate product readiness, and help the team detect defects early. They ensure releases meet acceptance criteria and reduce delivery risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage, and release readiness criteria
- Coordinate functional, integration, regression, and acceptance testing
- Track defects, risks, and quality trends through project delivery
- Partner with Developers on testability and fix validation
- Support Product Managers and Project Managers in go/no-go decisions

### Goals
- Improve confidence in product quality before release
- Reduce escaped defects and production issues
- Align testing with business-critical user outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, defect triage, and release readiness review
- Daily/weekly quality updates during delivery cycles
- Collaboration with product and engineering on acceptance criteria

### How this role interacts with others
- With Developers: reviews testability, helps triage defects, and validates fixes
- With Product Managers: confirms acceptance criteria and user impact are covered
- With Project Managers: informs risk, milestone readiness, and release timing decisions

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads shape experience quality, usability, and customer understanding. They ensure the product is intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Guide user research, design direction, and usability validation
- Translate customer needs into flows, prototypes, and interaction patterns
- Review alignment between product goals and implementation details
- Partner with delivery teams to balance speed, usability, and technical feasibility

### Goals
- Improve product usability and customer satisfaction
- Ensure design decisions are informed by evidence and user feedback
- Support consistent experience across features and releases

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews, customer research summaries, and usability findings
- Product planning sessions and backlog refinement
- Collaboration with engineering and QA on implementation validation

### How this role interacts with others
- With Product Managers: aligns design decisions to customer value and roadmap priorities
- With Developers: clarifies flows, edge cases, and implementation constraints
- With QA / Test Lead: validates usability and acceptance scenarios
- With Project Managers: helps sequence design work and flag dependencies

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide the system design, technical standards, and trade-off decisions that keep delivery sustainable and scalable.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture decisions, technical guardrails, and integration patterns
- Review design trade-offs, risks, and dependencies with delivery teams
- Guide implementation quality, maintainability, and performance standards
- Support estimation and sequencing for complex or cross-team work

### Goals
- Maintain a coherent and resilient technical foundation
- Reduce rework caused by avoidable architecture issues
- Enable delivery teams to move quickly without compromising quality

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews, technical planning sessions, and design discussions
- Risk and dependency updates during planning and execution
- Coordination across teams on integration and platform choices

### How this role interacts with others
- With Developers: provides technical direction, reviews implementation choices, and resolves design conflicts
- With Product Managers: helps assess feasibility, trade-offs, and timeline implications
- With Project Managers: identifies technical dependencies, sequencing risks, and escalation points

---

## Release / DevOps or Site Reliability Lead

### Role Summary
Release / DevOps or Site Reliability Leads ensure software can be deployed, monitored, and supported reliably in production. They reduce operational risk during delivery and release.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare deployment pipelines, environments, and release readiness checks
- Own observability, monitoring, on-call readiness, and rollback planning
- Coordinate smoke tests, production verification, and incident triage support
- Help the team manage infrastructure dependencies and operational risk

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and improve recovery time
- Increase service reliability and operational visibility
- Keep release and support processes consistent and repeatable

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists, deployment readiness reviews, and post-deploy verification updates
- Incident communication and operational health reviews
- Alignment with engineering and PMs on release scheduling and fallout management

### How this role interacts with others
- With Developers: validates build readiness, environments, and deployment quality
- With QA / Test Lead: aligns smoke testing and release validation
- With Project Managers: supports release windows, rollback planning, and stakeholder communication

---

## Security / Privacy Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads ensure products meet risk, compliance, and trust expectations. They help teams identify, mitigate, and communicate security concerns early.

### Responsibilities
- Review security and privacy requirements, threats, and vulnerabilities
- Support secure design reviews, controls, and remediation planning
- Coordinate compliance or data handling requirements with stakeholders
- Escalate issues affecting risk, trust, or enterprise obligations

### Goals
- Protect customer data and reduce delivery risk
- Embed security and privacy into product decisions early
- Improve confidence in compliance and enterprise readiness

### Typical Communication
- Threat reviews, compliance checkpoints, and risk escalation summaries
- Security review notes and remediation timelines
- Cross-functional updates during planning, release, and incident response

### How this role interacts with others
- With Technical Leads and Developers: reviews architecture choices, controls, and remediation work
- With Product Managers: helps evaluate business risk and acceptable trade-offs
- With Project Managers: tracks dependencies, escalations, and compliance-driven milestones

---

## Customer or Support Representative

### Role Summary
Customer or Support Representatives provide frontline insight from users, customers, or support teams. They help translate operational realities into product improvements and delivery decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Surface recurring customer pain points, support trends, and usage issues
- Share feedback on adoption, usability, and service quality
- Help define realistic acceptance scenarios and release risks
- Support communication around launch readiness and user impact

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and issue resolution quality
- Ensure delivery teams hear operational realities and customer impact
- Strengthen alignment between product decisions and support needs

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews, support issue summaries, and stakeholder updates
- Input to roadmap prioritization and release readiness discussions
- Escalation of issues affecting user trust or operational continuity

### How this role interacts with others
- With Product Managers: informs backlog priorities and customer problem framing
- With QA / Test Lead: shapes acceptance scenarios and validation checkpoints
- With Project Managers: helps communicate readiness, rollout impacts, and stakeholder concerns

---

## Business Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors and Executive Stakeholders provide strategic direction, investment support, and escalation authority for business-critical work.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business priorities, funding, and expected outcomes
- Support trade-off decisions that affect scope, timing, or resources
- Help resolve cross-functional escalations and strategic blockers
- Sponsor alignment between product outcomes and organizational goals

### Goals
- Ensure work remains aligned with business value and strategic priorities
- Support timely decisions across dependencies and competing demands
- Increase confidence in project outcomes and stakeholder trust

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone reviews, strategic status updates, and decision boards
- Escalation of priority, resource, or risk concerns
- Governance updates tied to business value and outcomes

### How this role interacts with others
- With Product Managers: aligns on outcomes, funding, and strategic priorities
- With Project Managers: supports governance, milestones, and escalations
- With Technical and Delivery teams: approves trade-offs when risk or business impact is significant

---

## Role interaction model

These roles work best when responsibilities are clear and handoffs are explicit. In most projects:
- Product Managers define the "why" and prioritize value
- Project Managers coordinate the "how" across schedule, communication, and risk
- Developers build the solution and validate technical execution
- QA / Test Leads protect release quality and acceptance readiness
- UX / Design Leads shape usability and customer experience
- Technical Leads / Architects guard maintainability and design coherence
- Release / DevOps or SRE Leads protect deployment reliability and operations
- Security / Privacy Leads reduce risk and compliance exposure
- Customer or Support Representatives bring user and service realities into planning
- Business Sponsors provide strategic direction and escalate decisions when needed

This model helps teams balance delivery speed with accountability, quality, and stakeholder alignment.
